
*"Sometimes a new dawn does not come with the light. It simply comes when we start looking towards the future again."*

**Description of the work**

*A New Dawn* was created in 2021, at a time still deeply marked by the consequences of the pandemic and a collective sense of isolation, uncertainty and limbo. The work does not directly address Covid, but rather conveys some of the feelings that period might have evoked: unease, a sense of heaviness, loneliness and the difficulty of clearly imagining what would come next.

The composition is dominated by black and white. In the lower part of the image, a dark surface emerges, criss-crossed by numerous streaks and marks that suggest a harsh, almost worn-out land. It is not a fertile and reassuring land, but a mental and visual landscape in which the weight of a difficult period seems to have settled.

Black plays a significant role and helps to create a sense of closure and depth. However, the streaks constantly break up this solidity, introducing movement and variation within the surface. It is as though, even in a seemingly motionless state, something continues to move.

It is precisely in this contrast that the title *A New Dawn* takes on its meaning. Dawn normally evokes light, rebirth and the beginning of a new day. Here, however, the possibility of a new beginning still seems uncertain and distant. Light does not immediately erase what has been experienced: it must emerge through a substance that is still dark and scarred.

The work can therefore be interpreted as the depiction of a moment of transition. There is no explicit promise of serenity, but rather the possibility that something might change. The ‘new dawn’ thus becomes an idea, a direction towards which to look even when the present still seems to offer no answers.

The choice of black and white reinforces this tension. By eliminating colour, the image sheds all decorative elements and focuses on the essential relationship between light and darkness, presence and absence, hope and unease. It is a deliberately powerful composition, in which the emotional dimension arises above all from contrast.

In this sense, *A New Dawn* is not necessarily a work about despair, but about the moment when hope is still fragile. It is the moment immediately before dawn, when we do not yet know whether what we are waiting for will actually arrive, but we continue nonetheless to turn our gaze towards that possibility.

**Technique and materials**

Work created in 2021 using acrylics on 300 g/m² card. The surface is constructed through the contrast between black and white and via a series of streaks and marks that define the lower part of the composition, contributing to the perception of a dark, scarred earth.

**Note on dimensions**

The original artwork measures **29 × 39 cm** and is presented with **a frame, mount and protective glass**, giving an overall size of **52 × 42 cm**.
