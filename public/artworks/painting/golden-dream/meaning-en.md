In the silence of the subconscious, images can emerge that seem to come from a space deeper than ordinary reality.
Interior visions that cannot be explained: they are traversed.

Meaning of the work
“Golden Dream” is born from a long personal research into the world of dreams, meditation, and interior images that emerge from the deepest layers of consciousness.
For me, the dream is not just a mental phenomenon, but a threshold through which the subconscious communicates through symbols, energies, and forms that are difficult to translate with rational language.
Many of the images that appear during sleep or in meditative states remain imprinted in the memory as luminous traces, intuitions, fragments of something that belongs to a broader dimension.
In this work, I wanted to represent the manifestation of a golden energetic core immersed in a purple, almost plasmatic space.
The central element emerges as a living presence, a sort of archetypal form or interior vibration that surfaces from the matter.
The contrast between the deep purple and the golden light suggests the encounter between the spiritual dimension and vital energy, between mystery and revelation.
“Golden Dream” represents a positive image for me:
an energetic apparition that brings with it transformation, interior openness, and the possibility of evolution.

Technique and matter
The work is created with mixed media on a recycled support, using the surface of an Ikea coffee table top transformed through material interventions and successive stratifications.
Vinyl glues, plaster, acrylics, and pure pigments build a vibrant and organic matter, while the concentric incisions of the central form generate a sense of energetic expansion and interior movement.
