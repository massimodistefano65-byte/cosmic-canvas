
*"The mind never proceeds in a straight line. Every thought generates another connection; every connection opens up a new path."*


**Description of the work**

*Neural Connections* was conceived as a metaphorical representation of the complexity of thought and the ceaseless activity of the mind. The work does not seek to reproduce the structure of the brain scientifically, but rather to visually translate that invisible network of impulses, associations, memories, intuitions and thoughts that are constantly forming and transforming within us.

The entire surface is criss-crossed by a dense network of lines that overlap, intersect and diverge into a multitude of paths. There is no real starting point, and there appears to be no ending point. Each line seems to generate another possibility, as if the material itself were permeated by ceaseless mental activity.

The particular technique used makes this network even more significant. Vinyl glue and plaster have been applied and moulded to create a structure that rises completely above the canvas. The connections are therefore not simply drawn on the surface: **they physically emerge from it**, transforming thought into a concrete, three-dimensional presence.

The silver, which covers almost the entire composition, accentuates the abstract and almost organic nature of the whole. Light glides over the numerous protrusions, enters the grooves and creates constantly shifting reflections and shadows. As one approaches the work, what may appear from a distance to be a single, compact structure reveals a surprising number of individual pathways.

It is precisely this multiplicity that constitutes one of the work’s most important aspects. The mind may appear to be a single system, but internally it is composed of a vast number of connections that interact continuously. One thought can trigger another, a memory can reawaken an emotion, an intuition can suddenly open up a completely different path.

The black and deep blue, present mainly along the edges, introduce a darker, more introspective dimension. The brightness of the silver does not erase this darkness, but coexists with it. This is a characteristic that also runs through much of the artist’s work: light does not necessarily eliminate darkness, but can emerge from within it.

*Neural Connections* thus becomes a sort of mental landscape. A complex, seemingly chaotic territory in which order and disorder coexist, and in which every path can lead towards another possibility.

The medium makes visible what normally remains invisible: **the ceaseless movement of the mind**.


**Technique and materials**

Work created in 2010 using mixed media on canvas. The surface was built up through a complex interweaving of vinyl glue and plaster, modelled to achieve a strongly three-dimensional, raised structure. The composition was subsequently given a predominantly silver finish, with the presence of black earth pigments and very deep shades of blue along the edges. The marked variation in reliefs, grooves and protrusions produces a continuous interplay with light, generating different shadows and reflections depending on the vantage point.
