Between collapse and rebirth, matter preserves traces of energy and transformation.
Every fracture can become a new beginning.

**The meaning of the artwork**
This work is born from a reflection on change and the continuous transformation of life.
Everything that seems stable can change suddenly: matter, emotions, inner balances, relationships, consciousness.
The forms present in the painting seem crossed by a constant tension, as if something were collapsing and regenerating at the same instant.
The black signs recall nerve connections, roots, or energetic paths; while the red areas represent vital nuclei, points of rupture but also possibilities of rebirth.
I did not want to represent a negative destruction, but the delicate moment in which change becomes necessary in order to evolve.
In this work, matter is not immobile: it reacts, retains memory, transforms continuously.
*"Everything Can Change"* speaks precisely of this: of the fragility of forms and of life's capacity to regenerate through change.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on a polystyrene panel, the work integrates acrylics, sprays, tar, and material substances that interact directly with the support.
The polystyrene is engraved, altered, and transformed through stratifications and gestural interventions that make the surface living, unstable, and organic, like matter in continuous evolution.
