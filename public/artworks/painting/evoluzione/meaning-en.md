*"To evolve means not merely to change, but to become progressively aware of who we are."*



**Description of the work**

*Evolution* stems from the depiction of a journey of growth that concerns not only the physical dimension of existence, but above all the inner one. The work takes shape from the idea that the most authentic evolution of the human being coincides with a progressive process of awareness, in which knowledge of the external world gradually gives way to self-knowledge.

At the centre of the composition, a vertical structure unfolds, growing progressively, almost like a staircase or a spine. The elements, initially smaller, increase in size and presence as they rise, creating the impression of continuous movement. This is not merely a matter of growth in space: that progression becomes a metaphor for a journey through different levels of consciousness.

The central structure is rendered in relief and physically extends beyond the surface of the panel. This three-dimensionality thus introduces a fundamental element: evolution is not merely an abstract concept, but acquires form, weight and substance. The coppery colour that characterises this form accentuates its presence and makes it resemble an organic structure, an element in transformation that passes through matter on its way towards a higher dimension.

Around this structure, concrete, plaster, earth, spray paint and acrylics build up an irregular, layered surface. The material seems to belong to a primordial phase, almost a state that is still incomplete, from which the central form gradually emerges. It is as though the work represented the transition from unconscious matter to conscious form.

The evolution evoked by the work can also be interpreted as a spiritual journey. Not necessarily in a religious sense, but as a gradual realisation of the Self. Each stage of the central structure may suggest a level of experience, an insight gained, or an inner transformation. The upward movement therefore does not indicate superiority over others, but a gradual drawing closer to one’s own essence.

In this sense, the work offers a deeply personal vision of evolution: not becoming something other than what we are, but gradually coming to recognise what we truly are. Growth thus becomes a process of shedding unawareness and acquiring consciousness.

*Evolution* is, ultimately, a material representation of the journey towards the Self. A path that does not necessarily follow a straight line and that requires time, experience and transformation, but which finds its direction in awareness. The form that emerges from the material thus becomes the symbol of a consciousness that, step by step, takes shape.



**Technique and materials**

Work created using mixed media on wood panel with concrete, plaster, earth pigments, spray paint and acrylic paints. The central structure is executed in strong three-dimensional relief, protruding approximately 1.5–2 cm from the surface, creating a striking sculptural effect. The layering of materials and the contrast between the textured surface and the copper-coloured relief accentuate the sense of growth and transformation in the work.
