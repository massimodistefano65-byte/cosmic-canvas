*Every genuine transformation leaves a wound. It is precisely through that wound that a new awareness can emerge.*

**Description of the work**

*Lacerazione Sublime* explores the moment when matter and being are pierced by a fracture that represents not merely a loss, but the beginning of a transformation. The laceration becomes the point at which what appeared solid opens up to a new possibility of existence.

The intense material structure, criss-crossed by incisions and reliefs, conveys the sensation of a surface marked by time and experience. Each mark seems to convey a tension, a transition, a force that profoundly alters the material without destroying it. At the centre of the work, the presence of red emerges as a vital core: a source of energy that continues to pulsate precisely at the site of the fracture.

The term *sublime* does not diminish the significance of the laceration, but elevates it. It suggests that even the most painful experiences can become tools for growth, transforming into opportunities for knowledge and inner evolution. There can be no true rebirth without something within us first being tested, challenged or broken.

The work invites us to view the wound not as a point of arrival, but as a threshold. It is through that opening that a deeper understanding of oneself and one’s own journey can emerge.

**Techniques and materials**

The work is created on a wooden panel through a complex process of engraving, which directly shapes the surface to create depth and relief. The polychromatic effect is achieved using acrylics and enamels, whilst the central translucent insert, reminiscent of stained glass, introduces a luminous element that interacts with the expressive power of the material.
