There exist thoughts, fears, and inner structures that over time stiffen within us.
Like matter that solidifies, preventing movement and transformation.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Cristallizzazioni"* is born from a reflection on the inner processes that tend to fix themselves in consciousness, transforming into blocks, mental habits, or attitudes that are difficult to overcome.
I was interested in representing that condition in which vital energy, instead of flowing freely, remains imprisoned in rigid inner structures that slow down personal and spiritual evolution.
The horizontal lines that cross the work recall sedimentations, stratified tensions, or waves frozen in time.
The surface appears crossed by a continuous struggle between movement and immobility, between transformation and fixation.
At the center, a luminous core emerges that represents for me the possibility of consciousness and change: an inner force that seeks to pass through the accumulated rigidities and reopen the flow of evolution.
The work is also born from a very personal perception of life, lived as a continuous tension between opposing forces: ascent and fall, openness and closure, matter and spirit.
*"Cristallizzazioni"* speaks precisely of this fragile balance:
of the risk of remaining imprisoned in one's own inner structures and of the constant need to transform to continue one's journey.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through deep material processes and thermal transformations of the surface.
Acrylics, earths, and metallic pigments interact with the support, creating engravings, stratifications, and horizontal lines that evoke processes of sedimentation and stiffening of the matter.
The use of heat alters and transforms the polystyrene, generating organic tensions and corroded surfaces, where the matter itself becomes a metaphor for inner conflict and the transformation of consciousness.
