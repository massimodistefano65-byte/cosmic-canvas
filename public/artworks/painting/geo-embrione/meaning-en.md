Between matter and consciousness, there exists an original space in which form is not yet completely defined.
A place of gestation, transformation, and inner birth.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Geo Embrione"* is born from a reflection on the origin of consciousness and on the deep bond between matter, energy, and inner transformation.
The central form appears deliberately suspended between different perceptual possibilities: it can recall an embryo, an organic presence, a face in formation, or an energetic core that slowly emerges from the matter.
I was interested in representing that primordial moment in which something begins to take shape without yet being completely defined.
A state of transition between the invisible and the visible, between potentiality and manifestation.
The work also speaks of the relationship between the human being and their own deepest dimension.
Matter is not experienced as an element separate from the spirit, but as a place of incarnation and transformation of consciousness.
The stratifications, erosions, and tensions of the surface evoke a continuous process of evolution, as if the form itself were trying to emerge from a still undefined dimension.
*"Geo Embrione"* therefore represents an inner birth:
the appearance of a presence that slowly seeks to recognize itself through matter.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through deep material processes and transformations of the support obtained through engravings, thermal alterations, and successive stratifications.
Glues, plaster, anilines, acrylics, and sprays interact, creating organic and vibrant surfaces, where matter appears in continuous metamorphosis.
The corroded textures and reliefs contribute to generating a sensation of embryonic formation and evolving energy, transforming the support into a living surface in constant transformation.
