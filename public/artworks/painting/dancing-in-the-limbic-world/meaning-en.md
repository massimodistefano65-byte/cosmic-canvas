There exists an inner dimension in which emotion, memory, and energy move even before becoming conscious thought.
An invisible territory where everything vibrates, connects, and transforms continuously.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Dancing in the Limbic World"* is born from a reflection on the limbic system, that deep part of the mind linked to emotions, primary impulses, memory, and the most instinctive reactions of the human being.
The work does not aim to represent the brain in a scientific way, but to evoke a mental and energetic landscape in continuous movement.
The lines seem like neural connections, organic networks, or energy flows that cross a liquid and suspended space, while the circular forms evoke cells, impulses, signals, or presences in transformation.
The lower part, crossed by incandescent tones, represents for me a sort of primary energy: an inner fire from which emotions, memories, and states of consciousness emerge.
I was interested in creating a sensation of unstable balance, as if everything were in a continuous dance between order and chaos, lucidity and intuition, matter and perception.
The work speaks of that invisible dimension that constantly inhabits us and that often guides our life much more than rational thought.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through intense material processes and thermal transformations of the surface.
Anilines, sprays, enamels, and interventions with heat alter the support, creating cavities, reliefs, and organic tensions that recall cellular structures and neural connections.
The matter is thus transformed into a living and pulsating surface, where sign, light, and deformation merge into a single energetic flow.
