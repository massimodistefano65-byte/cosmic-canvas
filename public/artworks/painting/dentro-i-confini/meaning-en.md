
*"We live within boundaries that sometimes protect us, sometimes restrict us, and often force us to ask ourselves who drew them."*

**Description of the work**

*Within Boundaries* stems from a reflection on the condition of the individual within society and on the ambivalent relationship each of us may have with the limits imposed upon us. The work does not simply present a contrast between freedom and rule, but highlights a more complex contradiction: some boundaries are necessary for us to live together, whilst others can become instruments of control and restriction.

The composition is built around two large red forms positioned at either end of the image. They almost resemble two bastions, two structures that delimit and enclose the central space. Between them lies a greyer, more irregular area, criss-crossed by marks, grooves and reddish transverse stripes. It is precisely this intermediate space that becomes the symbolic place in which the individual finds themselves living and moving.

The red introduces a strong and assertive presence. The two lateral masses seem to exert pressure on the central space, suggesting the existence of limits that do not necessarily depend on our will. The stripes crossing the central area, on the other hand, seem to indicate overlapping paths, divisions and directions, like a series of rules and constraints by which we are constantly called upon to find our bearings.

The central grey area thus takes on a particular significance. It is a space of transition, but also a space of compromise. It is the place where individual freedom must engage with its surroundings. It is not entirely free, yet neither is it entirely confined: it is a space in which every choice is confronted by a series of pre-existing limits.

The concept of a boundary, however, is not presented solely in a negative light. Rules, laws and the principles of good living represent necessary boundaries, because without a shared standard, one person’s freedom could turn into a restriction on another’s freedom. The problem arises when the boundary ceases to be a tool for balance and becomes an imposition that restricts a person’s space beyond what is necessary.

It is precisely this ambivalence that forms the conceptual core of the work. Within what boundaries do we live? Which have we consciously chosen, and which, on the other hand, have we simply accepted because they were imposed on us? And above all, to what extent is it possible to remain true to oneself when the context in which we live continually dictates what we can or cannot be?

*Within the Boundaries* does not offer a definitive answer. Rather, it invites us to recognise the limits that surround us and to distinguish between those that make coexistence possible and those that can become a form of conditioning. From this perspective, the boundary is not merely a line that separates: it is also something that defines the space within which we can exist.

**Technique and materials**

Work created in 2019 using acrylics and enamels on 300 g/m² card. The composition is characterised by the contrast between the large red elements at the sides, the transverse stripes and the grey, heavily textured central area.

**Note on dimensions**

The original work measures **29 × 39 cm** and is presented with **a frame, mount and protective glass**, giving an overall dimension of **52 × 42 cm**.
