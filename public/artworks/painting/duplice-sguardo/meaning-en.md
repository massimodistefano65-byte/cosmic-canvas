Within every being, different identities, tensions, and inner states coexist.
Sometimes it is enough to change perspective to see a completely different face emerge.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Double Glance"* is born from a reflection on the duality present in human nature and on the continuous transformation of perception.
The work was conceived to profoundly modify its expression when rotated 180 degrees.
The change is not only visual: what emerges is a different psychological and emotional presence, almost a second identity hidden within the first.
I was interested in creating an unstable image, capable of suggesting how within every individual, forces, aspects, and states of being often opposite to each other coexist.
Light and shadow, openness and closure, awareness and bewilderment can continuously alternate, modifying the way we perceive ourselves and others.
The work also speaks of the relativity of the gaze.
What we see is never definitive: it changes along with our inner state, the perspective, and the consciousness with which we observe.
In *"Double Glance"*, the face thus becomes a symbol of an identity in continuous oscillation, suspended between different possibilities of being.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on a worked polystyrene panel, the work is born through pictorial interventions, thermal alterations, and material processes of the surface.
Acrylics, sprays, and deformations of the support create a dynamic structure designed to transform the perception of the image through the rotation of the work itself.
The irregular and changing surface contributes to reinforcing the sense of perceptual instability and continuous inner transformation.
