In the silence of matter, there exists an invisible center from which everything originates and toward which everything returns.
Every being is both a fragment and a totality at the same time.

**The meaning of the artwork**

*"Coscienza"* is born from the attempt to give a visible form to a deep inner state: that condition in which the individual stops perceiving themselves as a separate entity and begins to recognize themselves as part of something broader.
The entire composition develops around a central core from which a continuous circular movement propagates, similar to an energetic wave or a cosmic vibration.
For me, this movement represents consciousness itself: an energy that expands, connects, and passes through everything without losing the bond with its own origin.
The work does not want to describe a philosophical concept in a rational way, but to evoke a perception.
That subtle sensation of belonging to the Whole that sometimes emerges in silence, in meditation, or in moments of deep inner presence.
The matter, with its stratifications, cracks, and reliefs, becomes an integral part of this research:
not a simple surface, but a memory of time, transformation, a trace of the human experience.
*"Coscienza"* represents for me the encounter between matter and spirit, between terrestrial roots and inner expansion.
An invitation to go beyond the limit of the ego to perceive the hidden unity that connects all things.

**Technique and material**
The work is created with material technique on canvas through the use of earths, plaster, acrylics, enamels, and metallic inserts that interact, creating a vibrant and stratified surface.
The circular processes and material overlaps generate a continuous expansive movement, while the presence of metallic reflections and gold leaf amplifies the perception of inner light and energetic depth.
