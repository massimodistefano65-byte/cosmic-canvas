Technique: acrylics and spray paint on 300 g/m² card



*“Energy is not merely movement: it is the invisible force that runs through our very being and drives us to transform what we feel.”*



**Description of the artwork**

*Red Energy* belongs to the *Energy* series and stems from a desire to give visible form to an intense and concentrated perception of the life force. Red dominates the composition as a physical, almost palpable presence, capable of immediately conveying a sensation of power, warmth and movement.

Concentrations of red traverse the surface of the work, creating a predominantly horizontal movement. The colour does not gather at a single focal point, but expands and spreads throughout the entire composition, generating the perception of a diffuse, intense and constantly shifting energy. The choice of red accentuates this sensation. It is a colour that instinctively evokes vitality, impulse, passion and transformation, but here it takes on, above all, the meaning of a constructive energy. It is not a destructive or chaotic force: it is a positive drive, an inner reserve that can fuel change and growth.

The layers of acrylic and spray paint help to create a dynamic surface, in which the colour seems to move and continually generate new vibrations. The variations in density, the nuances and the overlapping traces make the image feel almost like a phenomenon in the making, closer to an energetic experience than to the representation of a concrete object.

In *Energia Rossa*, energy can also be interpreted as an inner state. It is that force which is sometimes felt without being able to be defined rationally, a presence that flows through the body and mind and which can become a creative impulse, awareness and the capacity for transformation.

The work therefore does not seek to explain energy, but to **make it perceptible**. The central core becomes the focal point of a force that belongs simultaneously to matter and to the inner self, suggesting that what we perceive within ourselves can find a visible form through art.



**Technique and materials**

Work created on 300 g/m² card using acrylics and spray paint. The layering and overlapping of colours create effects of depth, luminosity and movement, accentuating the sensation of concentration and the radiating energy.



**Presentation**

The artwork is supplied ready for display, complete with a mat and frame. The dimensions of the artwork are 29 × 39 cm, whilst the overall dimensions including the passe-partout and frame are 42 × 52 cm. Support: 300 g/m² card
