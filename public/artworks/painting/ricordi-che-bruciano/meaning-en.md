A powerful depiction of inner conflict, where the weight of memory is transformed into incandescent energy and visual catharsis.


### Meaning of the artwork

*Burning Memories* explores the intimate and sometimes painful dimension of memory, understood not merely as an archive of the past, but as a living, pulsating substance in a state of constant combustion. The work takes the form of a conceptual space in which the resurfacing of memories generates a veritable emotional explosion. The central band, dominated by a red trail of extraordinary Dionysian intensity, cuts through the composition, evoking the process of inner combustion: the fire that burns, purifies and, at the same time, leaves an indelible mark on the psyche.

The expressive tension arises from the constant dialogue between the deep darkness of the lower section – characterised by a dense and tormented graphic grid that evokes mental sedimentation and the scars of lived experience – and the flashes of turquoise that emerge in the upper section. This visual dialectic transforms the pain of memory into a creative and cathartic act: the burning becomes the necessary means to dissolve the weight of the past, releasing a powerful energy of awareness and renewal.


### Technique and materials

The work is executed on heavyweight 300 g/m² card, a rigid, textured surface capable of supporting a complex layering of elements. The dynamic gestural quality of the acrylic painting blends with the rapid, instinctive misting of the spray, creating striking contrasts in finish and visual depth. The incised graphic grooves and the dense chiaroscuro of the lower section contrast with the pure, textured saturation of the red pigment and the blue glazes.
