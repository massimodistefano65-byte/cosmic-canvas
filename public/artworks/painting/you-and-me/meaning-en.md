Every authentic encounter is an invisible bridge between two worlds that choose to recognize each other.

**Description of the work**
*"YOU and ME"* is born from a reflection on the deep bond that can unite two human beings. It does not tell a love story in a narrative sense, but that silent force that makes encounter, sharing, and mutual recognition possible.
The composition develops along a horizontal axis that accompanies the gaze toward the center of the work, where a light emerges as a point of balance between the two extremities. It is not a descriptive light, but the symbol of what is born when two individualities truly enter into a relationship: a common space, made of listening, trust, and presence.
The forms, crossed by fluid movements and material stratifications, suggest that every human relationship is a process in continuous evolution. No relationship remains immobile: it grows, changes, passes through moments of harmony and tension, just as happens in life.
While addressing a different theme compared to my other works, *"YOU and ME"* also speaks of research. In this case, however, the research is not directed toward individual interiority, but toward that mysterious balance that arises from the encounter between two people, reminding us that love represents one of the deepest experiences of our existence.

**Techniques and materials**
The work is created on canvas using a mixed media technique that combines glues, acrylic colors, and sprays. The processing of the surface generates reliefs and stratifications that dialogue with light, giving depth to the composition and accompanying the visual movement toward the central core of the work.
