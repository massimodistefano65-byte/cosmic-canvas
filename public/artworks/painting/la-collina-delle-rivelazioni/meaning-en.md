Nature does not offer answers: it creates the silence necessary for them to emerge.

**Description of the work**
*"La collina delle rivelazioni"* is born from a period of my life in which contact with nature was daily and deep. Long walks through woods, hills, and paths became opportunities for listening, moments in which the external landscape seemed to reflect the internal one.
The work does not represent a real hill, but a symbolic place: a space of the soul in which thought slows down and lets intuitions surface that often remain hidden in the frenzy of life. The forms seem modeled by time and natural elements, as if the matter safeguarded an ancient memory ready to reveal itself to those who know how to observe carefully.
The lines and surfaces guide the gaze along a path that invites contemplation more than interpretation. Revelation is not a sudden or spectacular event, but a subtle understanding that arises from the silent dialogue between the human being and nature.
This work tells of the value of walking not only as movement in space, but as an inner experience. Every step can become an opportunity to find oneself, when one is willing to listen to what the landscape suggests.

**Technique and material**
The work is created on a polystyrene panel, modeled through a personal technique of subtraction of matter by means of heat. The engravings and reliefs are obtained without adding material, transforming a perfectly flat surface into a three-dimensional texture rich in depth. The coloring with acrylics and enamels enhances the material structure, accentuating the dialogue between light, shadow, and texture.
