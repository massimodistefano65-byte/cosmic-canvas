**The meaning of the artwork**
*Searching for You* is born from a very personal need for inner research related to the figure of my father and the deep desire to recover memories, sensations, and emotional fragments left suspended in time.
For years I felt the sensation of an incomplete memory, as if an important part of the lived experience had remained hidden in the deepest layers of consciousness.
Also through paths of introspection and practices related to deep memory, I tried to cross these inner territories in an attempt to find a presence that never really disappeared.
The central form of the work appears suspended between emergence and dissolution, almost like an image that slowly tries to resurface from the subconscious.
Around it, organic lines develop similar to roots, connections, or energetic filaments that cross both the upper and lower parts of the composition.
These elements represent for me the invisible bonds of memory and inner experience: deep traces that continue to connect the human being to what they have lived, loved, or lost.
The work therefore speaks of the search for a presence that continues to exist within us, even when we can no longer clearly define it through conscious memory.
*Searching for You* thus becomes the attempt to reconstruct an inner contact with something that time has not really managed to erase.



**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on canvas, the work is born through material processes, incisions, and transformations of the surface obtained through successive stratifications and alterations of the support.
Vinyl glues, plaster, earths, sprays, and acrylics interact, creating organic textures and visual depths that accentuate the sensation of fragmented memory and a presence slowly emerging.
The branched lines and corroded surfaces contribute to generating an atmosphere suspended between apparition and dissolution, reinforcing the sense of inner research that runs through the entire work.
