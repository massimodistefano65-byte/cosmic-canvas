The mind is never still.
Thoughts, memories, and inner energies continuously intertwine, transforming over time, passing through states of density, conflict, and openness.

Meaning of the work
“Evolving Thoughts” is born from a reflection on the continuous movement of consciousness and the way in which thoughts accumulate, intertwine, and transform within the human experience.
The lower part of the work appears dense, intricate, almost suffocated by a tangle of lines and matter that evoke mental chaos, emotional accumulation, and the complexity of inner states.
From this initial concentration, however, a progressive lightening of the structure emerges: the forms slowly begin to open, expand, and dissolve upwards.
I was interested in representing thought as a living organism, in continuous metamorphosis.
Not something stable or defined, but an energetic flow that constantly evolves, passing through tensions, transformations, and moments of clarification.
The vertical progression of the composition also suggests an inner journey:
a transition from density to lightness, from unconscious accumulation toward a more open form of awareness.
The work therefore speaks of the continuous evolution of the mind and consciousness, but also of the human being's difficulty in finding balance within the incessant movement of their own thoughts.
“Evolving Thoughts” represents the attempt to transform inner chaos into a passage, research, and the possibility of growth.

Technique and matter
Created on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through a process of subtraction and direct transformation of the matter through the use of heat.
The surface is thermally worked until it generates incisions, reliefs, and organic structures that emerge from the support itself like a low relief.
The intertwined lines and scrolls present in the work are therefore not obtained through material additions, but through the modification and shaping of the polystyrene by means of fire.
This process allows the matter to take on a living, dynamic, and almost continuously expanding appearance, accentuating the sense of inner movement that runs through the composition.
The chromatic intervention with acrylics and anilines dialogues with the depths and surfaces created by the heat, generating luminous vibrations and perceptual variations that reinforce the idea of evolution and transformation of consciousness.
