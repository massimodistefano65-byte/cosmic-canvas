
A material explosion of the dreamlike realm, where the three-dimensional memory of the subconscious surfaces amidst swathes of heat, metallic glints and chromatic layers.


### Meaning of the work

*Unforgettable dream* presents itself as a visual threshold into the intangible yet persistent realm of indelible dreams. The work does not attempt to depict a figurative dream image, but transforms the experience of dreaming into pure, layered matter. It is the emergence of an unconscious memory that refuses to fade upon waking, leaving a deep and tangible imprint on the consciousness. The composition unfolds as an ever-evolving inner landscape: a dense network of grooves and flaking marks the surface, evoking the invisible maps of memory and the deepest recesses of the psyche.

The expressive tension lies in the dynamic balance between the warm, vibrant fields of colour — ranging from shades of burnt copper to earthy reds — and the broad, mineral-like, bluish glazes that float in the background. This contrast in colour and texture symbolises the tension between the emotional intensity of a dreamlike vision and the suspended distance of time. The work thus becomes a catalyst for introspection, where every groove and every reflection invites the viewer to trace the fragments of their own inner experience.


### Technique and materials

The work exploits the sculptural qualities of 3 cm-thick polystyrene, shaped through a hot-modelling process that hollows out and alters its original structure. Layers of acrylic paints, metallic pigments and binders are applied to this three-dimensional, uneven surface, highlighting the material’s porosity and the depth of the grooves. The incised areas catch the light in an iridescent manner, giving the work a distinct tactile and sculptural presence.
