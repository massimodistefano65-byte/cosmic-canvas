
A cosmic explosion of opposing forces, where earthly matter meets primordial fire and the frequencies of the invisible.


### Meaning of the work

With *Tumulto energetico*, I sought to capture the exact moment when the primordial forces of matter and spirit collide and merge. The work is a battlefield and a place of creation: the warm hues of red and parched earth surge down from above like a glowing mass, whilst a band of deep blue and teal cuts across the composition like a cosmic breath or a wave of purifying frequency.

In the lower section, a complex web of intertwined roots and luminous filaments etches the surface, symbolising the search for anchorage and the fundamental structure that underpins this apparent chaos. It is a turmoil that does not destroy, but generates new life and new awareness through the pure vibration of colour and organic matter.


### Technique and materials

On a surface measuring 124x80 cm, I have combined the use of natural earth pigments with the fluidity and intensity of acrylics and the gestural quality of spray paint. The layered overlaps and the graininess of the earth pigments lend the work a strong visual and tactile presence, accentuating the contrast between the arid porosity of the background and the translucent grooves of the intertwined lines.
