The deepest trials of existence do not always manifest with the noise of the storm, but with the slow and incessant friction of being against life.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Il tormento del vivere"* tells of that silent tension that accompanies the human journey: the continuous confrontation between the desire to rise and the weight of earthly experience. There is no narrative representation, but a matter that seems to consume and regenerate itself at the same instant.
From the background emerge stratifications, glazes, and abrasions that recall ancient surfaces, while red bursts in as a living presence, impossible to ignore. It does not completely invade the space, but crosses it, like an inner force that resurfaces despite everything. It is the symbol of a suffering that does not destroy, but rather transforms.
The work suggests that torment is not only pain, but also the price of awareness. Every fold of the surface seems to safeguard the sign of time, of lived experiences, and of continuous inner metamorphoses. It is precisely in this tension that the possibility of a spiritual evolution is born: crossing the conflict without being annihilated by it.
Abstraction leaves the viewer the freedom to recognize their own experience, transforming the painting into a space for personal reflection rather than a simple image to observe.

**Technique and material**
Created in 2020 on 300 g/m² artistic cardstock (30 × 40 cm), the work employs acrylic colors, sprays, and natural earths. The surface, although devoid of added matter, presents a marked materiality obtained through folding, processing, and chromatic layering that confer tactile depth and a rich luminous vibration.
The work is offered complete with a frame with passe-partout and protective glass, available in black, white, or light wood finishes, reaching an overall dimension of approximately 52 × 42 cm.
