Matter preserves invisible memories, ancient signs that continue to live beneath the surface of time.
Traces interior, archaeologies of being.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Ancient Traces"* is born from a reflection on deep memory and the invisible traces that every experience leaves within us.
I was interested in creating a work that seemed to emerge from an undefined time, like a found fragment, consumed and transformed by matter and energy.
The engravings, the circular signs, and the stratifications evoke archaic symbols, inner maps, or artifacts belonging to a forgotten collective memory.
I did not want to represent something precise, but to suggest the presence of an ancient and universal language that continues to exist beneath the surface of consciousness.
For me, this work speaks of the relationship between time, matter, and inner memory.
Every engraved sign becomes a trace of passage, a silent testimony of something that continues to transform without disappearing completely.
The surface appears corrupted, hollowed out, oxidized, but precisely through this transformation, a new form of energy and presence emerges.
*"Ancient Traces"* represents the hidden memory of things:
that which time modifies, but does not manage to completely erase.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through engravings, thermal alterations, and material stratifications that transform the support into a surface similar to an organic artifact.
Acrylic colors, earths, chromatic oxidations, and interventions on the matter create corroded and sedimented textures, where the sign appears engraved into the surface like an archaeological or energetic trace suspended between memory and transformation.
