There exist inner places where time ceases to be a simple succession and becomes a silent presence, suspended between memory, transformation, and permanence.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Dentro il tempo"* is born from a reflection on the relationship between consciousness, memory, and the temporal dimension.
I was interested in representing time not as a linear movement, but as an inner space to be traversed.
A suspended place where traces of the past, deep perceptions, and states of being continue to coexist beyond normal chronological experience.
The central form appears as an immobile and silent presence, almost an ancient fragment kept within a dimension outside of time.
It is not completely defined: it can evoke a threshold, an archetypal structure, or a memory slowly emerging.
The composition is crossed by concentric and enveloping forms that recall a spiral or a shell.
The lines accompany the gaze toward an ever-deeper interior depth, creating the sensation of a slow crossing of memory and consciousness.
I was interested in suggesting the idea of an inner descent: a progressive approach to a hidden core of being, where experience, time, and perception seem to stratify silently.
Unlike other more dynamic and conflictual works, here the research focuses more on suspension, essentiality, and inner listening.
Emptiness, light, and matter dialogue in balance, generating a contemplative and rarefied dimension.
*"Dentro il tempo"* therefore represents an inner space of permanence and depth, where matter seems to safeguard invisible traces of human experience and time becomes a threshold toward something deeper and more original.

**Technique and material**
Created with mixed media through the use of plaster, vinyl glues, and acrylics, the work is born from a process of controlled stratification and essential processing of the surface.
The light textures, delicate reliefs, and concentric forms contribute to creating a suspended and enveloping matter, capable of evoking a contemplative dimension outside of time.
Light interacts with the surfaces in a soft and silent way, accentuating the sensation of inner immersion and of memory safeguarded in the matter.
