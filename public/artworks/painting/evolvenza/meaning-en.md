Mixed media on canvas – 20 x 20 cm
Vinyl glues, plaster, textured acrylics.

“Evolvenza” is a small work that condenses, within a minimalist space, one of the most recurrent themes in Massimo Di Stefano’s artistic practice: the inner movement of consciousness towards continuous transformation.

The image unfolds like an organic vortex, a spiral that seems to expand whilst simultaneously contracting towards a bright red, pulsating, almost embryonic central core. That centre appears as a spark of primal energy, a point of origin or rebirth around which everything takes shape.
The composition suggests a non-linear, circular evolutionary process. Each ring seems to represent a transition, a layering of experience, a passage through matter and time. The spiral thus becomes a symbol of inner growth, but also of a constant return to oneself: a movement that never proceeds in a definitive manner, but continues to transform.

From a material perspective, the work is constructed through an intense three-dimensional process achieved using vinyl glues, plaster and acrylics. The relief surfaces create an almost organic structure, resembling a natural formation, a fossilised shell, or a cellular or cosmic vortex. The material is not simply painted, but modelled and physically shaped, becoming an integral part of the work’s meaning.
In ‘Evolvenza’, two opposing yet complementary forces coexist: the drive towards ascent and the pull towards the original core. It is a vision of existence as a ceaseless process of transformation, in which the human being remains suspended between memory, evolution and spiritual quest.
