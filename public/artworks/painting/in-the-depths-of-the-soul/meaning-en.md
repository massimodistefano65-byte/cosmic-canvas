In the inner depths, there are forms, memories, and energies that rarely emerge to the surface.
A silent and submerged universe that continues to live within us.

**The meaning of the artwork**

*In the Depths of the Soul* is born from a reflection on the subconscious and the invisible layers of the human psyche.
I was interested in exploring that deep dimension in which emotions, memories, intuitions, and inner presences accumulate over time, forming a sort of organic and mysterious landscape.
The structure of the work seems observed through a microscopic or cosmic lens at the same time: cells, planets, membranes, biological fragments, and suspended forms coexist in an undefined space, as if the inner microcosm and the outer universe were part of the same reality.
The central red element represents for me a vital core, an energetic presence that emerges from the silent chaos of the subconscious.
It is not a completely defined form, but something in continuous transformation, as happens to the deep contents of consciousness when they try to surface.
The work speaks of inner immersion, but also of listening:
of the necessity to cross one's own depths without fear, accepting that within us coexist fragility, memory, energy, and the possibility of transformation.

**Technique and material**

Created with mixed media on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through material processes that transform the support into an organic and stratified low relief.
Acrylics, earths, and interventions on the surface create a vibrant texture made of cavities, reliefs, and sedimentations, where the matter takes on an almost cellular or cosmic appearance, amplifying the sensation of immersion in the inner depths.
