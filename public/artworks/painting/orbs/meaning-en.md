
*A visual threshold between the visible world and the invisible realms, where matter cracks to allow light to emerge.*

**Description of the work**

In *Orbs*, the pictorial surface is transformed into a field of vibrational resonance where solid matter seems to dissolve, giving way to phenomena of pure energy. The work is dominated by a sculptural, corrugated texture, criss-crossed by dynamic lines of force that evoke cosmic currents or subtle flows invisible to the human eye. Against this deep, textured background, painted in ethereal shades of teal, copper, earth and leaden tones, golden circles of various sizes emerge, floating along a diagonal trajectory.

The title makes explicit reference to *orbs*: luminous, globular entities that hover on the border between visual perception and subtle mystery. Without dwelling on mere literal iconography, the work evokes those energetic presences often associated with spiritual manifestations, souls in transit, or the enigmatic spheres of light that traverse the skies of ufological research and unidentified phenomena.

These metallic, golden spheres act as catalysts for light and consciousness. They are not mere geometric shapes, but veritable nodes of frequency: droplets of intelligence or energetic memory that traverse the density of the material fabric. The artist thus creates a visual threshold between the visible world and the invisible realms, leaving the viewer with the sensation of witnessing the precise moment when the invisible reveals itself in matter.

**Technique and materials**

Through a multi-material approach characterised by high plastic relief, Di Stefano shapes the surface by creating grooves, ripples and surface tensions. The synergistic use of glazes, natural pigments, metallic copper tones and shades of teal interacts with the spherical lustre of the golden *orbs*, transforming the materiality of the canvas into a veritable 3D vibrational map.
