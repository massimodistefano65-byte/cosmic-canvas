There are moments when change is not yet visible, but can be clearly perceived as a tension in the air, an underground movement that precedes transformation.

**The meaning of the artwork**
*"Something is Happening"* is born from the perception of a state of imminent transformation.
That subtle and restless sensation that precedes an event, an inner change, or a shift in emotional and perceptual balance.
The work is crossed by a dynamic and irregular structure that seems to expand in space like an unstable energetic field.
The circular forms and the tensions of the surface evoke cells, vibrations, or nuclei in movement, as if something were slowly taking shape beneath the visible matter.
I was interested in representing that suspended moment that precedes the manifestation of something new:
a sort of inner storm in preparation, where energy, intuition, and transformation begin to move even before becoming fully conscious.
The composition deliberately maintains an unresolved tension.
Nothing appears stable or definitive: everything seems crossed by a process of continuous mutation.
The work therefore speaks of change as an energetic and emotional state, of those deep transformations that begin silently within us even before taking on a clear and recognizable form.
*"Something is Happening"* represents the intuitive perception of an invisible movement already in progress.

**Technique and material**
Created on a polystyrene panel, the work is born through a direct processing of the surface through the use of heat.
The matter is transformed without external additions, modifying the support itself until obtaining reliefs, cavities, and organic structures that generate an irregular and vibrant surface despite starting from an originally flat board.
The heat models the polystyrene, creating visual tensions, expansions, and circular forms that accentuate the sense of energy in movement present in the work.
The chromatic intervention with acrylics, glues, sand, and anilines amplifies the depth of the surface and contributes to creating a suspended and dynamic atmosphere, crossed by a sensation of imminent transformation.
