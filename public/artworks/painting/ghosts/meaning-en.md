*"There are presences that never reveal themselves fully: they remain on the fringes of our vision, suspended between what we see and what we may merely have sensed."*



**Description of the work**

*Ghosts* stems from a body of work in which painting becomes a liminal space between visible reality and the invisible realm. Created in 2010, it belongs to a particularly significant period in my artistic journey, when the relationship between matter, perception and the inner world began to take on an increasingly prominent role in my work.

Upon close observation of the work’s surface, barely recognisable figures emerge: faces, presences, features that seem to surface from the material without ever fully revealing themselves. They are not defined images, but apparitions that the eye must slowly recognise. Some of these figures seem to have unsettling expressions, almost like smirks, creating an ambivalent sensation: what may initially appear to be a simple abstract pattern gradually transforms into something that seems to be looking back at us.

It is precisely this ambiguity that constitutes one of the central elements of the work. The faces might actually be present, or they might be the result of the mind’s particular ability to recognise shapes and figures within random structures. But, once we have identified them, it becomes difficult not to keep seeing them. The work thus becomes a perceptual experience in which the boundary between imagination and presence is deliberately blurred.

The title *Ghosts* evokes this liminal dimension. Ghosts are not necessarily depicted as concrete figures: they may be traces, memories, energies or presences that seem to linger in a space even when we cannot prove their existence. In my work, this interest in what eludes ordinary perception is also intertwined with a personal sensitivity towards the most mysterious and inexplicable aspects of human experience.

The painterly medium helps to convey this sensation. The layers, the earth pigments, the chalk and the colour build a surface that does not appear entirely stable: it almost seems to harbour something within it. The figures are not simply painted onto the surface, but seem to emerge from it, as if they were trapped within the material and were merely waiting for the right gaze to reveal themselves.

*Ghosts* is therefore a work that does not seek to explain the mystery, but to make it perceptible. It is an invitation to look a little longer, because what appears random may suddenly take on a form, a face, a presence. And perhaps it is precisely at that moment—when we can no longer distinguish with certainty between what we have seen and what our mind has made us see—that the work finds its deepest meaning.



**Technique and materials**

The work is created using mixed media on panel, employing acrylics, gesso and earth pigments. The layering of materials contributes to the creation of a textured and irregular surface, in which forms and presences seem to emerge gradually from the material.
