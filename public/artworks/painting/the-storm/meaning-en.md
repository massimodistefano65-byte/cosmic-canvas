**Description of the work**
*"The Storm"* represents that suspended instant in which everything is changing.
It is not yet the explosion of the event, but the moment that precedes it: when the air becomes charged with electricity, the sky holds its breath, and every element seems to prepare for an inevitable transformation.
The circular forms scattered across the surface recall cells, drops, energetic nuclei, or particles in continuous aggregation. Nothing is immobile. Every element seems to respond to an invisible force that crosses the work and continuously modifies its balance.
As often happens in my artistic research, the external landscape becomes a metaphor for an inner state. The storm is that which crosses consciousness when old structures begin to give way and something new attempts to emerge.
I do not consider chaos as a negative element. Every authentic transformation requires a phase of instability. It is precisely in disorder that reality finds new possibilities to organize itself and to evolve.
The work therefore invites one to inhabit that moment of apparent uncertainty without opposing it, recognizing in the storm a creative rather than a destructive force. Every deep change is born from a fracture of the previous balance, and only by crossing that threshold is it possible to access a new form of awareness.

**Technique and material**
Mixed media on canvas. The work is created through the layering of acrylics, enamels, and sands, materials that build a slightly textured surface, rich in vibrations and nuances. The textures do not have a purely decorative function, but participate in the construction of the image, suggesting the continuous movement of the energies that cross the composition and amplifying the sensation of a transformation in progress.
