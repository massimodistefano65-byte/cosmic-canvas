The depths of consciousness safeguard forms that escape reason, but that intimately belong to the being.

**Description of the work**
*"Incubo"* explores the most enigmatic territory of inner experience: that in which fears take shape and the unconscious becomes visible matter. The image seems to emerge from a space suspended between the organic and the mental, like a presence born from the depths of the psyche without ever assuming a defined identity.
The large black forms in relief envelop and compress the composition, creating a continuous tension between empty and full, light and darkness. They do not represent a recognizable monster, but the very sensation of the nightmare: something that looms, that transforms incessantly, and that escapes every attempt at control.
The almost total absence of color amplifies the sense of suspension, letting the matter, the shadows, and the three-dimensionality tell of the inner conflict. The viewer is invited to confront what normally remains hidden, discovering how even the darkest areas of consciousness can reveal their own expressive force.
Rather than describing a fear, the work restores the universal experience of the encounter with the deepest and most unfathomable part of oneself.

**Technique and material**
Created in 2010 on canvas, the work employs a mixed media technique based on plaster, vinyl glues, earths, and acrylic colors. The three-dimensional relief is built through the modeling of the matter, which generates marked volumes and organic surfaces. The black and white monochrome accentuates the play of light and shadow, giving the composition a strong plastic intensity and an almost sculptural presence.
