

*"Inside the heart there is not only what we love. There is everything that we are."*



**Description of the work**

*Inside the Heart* stems from a reflection on the heart, understood not merely as an organ, but as one of the most profound symbols of the human experience. The heart represents what we feel, what we desire, what we remember and what, often, we cannot express in words. It is the imaginary place where emotions, passions, wounds, desires and contradictions coexist.

The work does not depict a recognisable heart in a figurative sense. Instead, it evokes it through a complex, enveloping structure, in which forms and lines seem to intersect and continually generate new paths. Looking ‘inside the heart’ therefore means entering an inner space in which there is not necessarily a definitive order.

Turquoise is one of the most evocative colours in the composition. Its luminosity introduces a fluid and profound, almost aquatic dimension, which contrasts with and, at the same time, interacts with the other shades. Through this colour, the image acquires a particular sense of breathing space and openness.

The golden lines criss-crossing the surface, on the other hand, add a luminous and symbolic dimension. They intersect, overlap and seem to form a network of connections. They can be perceived as thoughts, emotions, experiences and relationships that constantly flow through a person’s inner life.

In this sense, the heart depicted in the work is not necessarily a place of peace. It is a living space, where harmony and unease, desire and fear, openness and defence can coexist. The complexity of the image reflects precisely this impossibility of reducing inner experience to a single emotion.

Although the surface is made of polystyrene, it remains deliberately smooth and devoid of any textural treatment. Complexity is therefore not achieved through the relief or physicality of the medium, but through colour, transparencies and the interweaving of lines. Aniline dyes help to maintain this fluid and luminous quality, making the image appear as though it were almost traversed by internal currents.

*Inside the Heart* can ultimately be interpreted as a sort of journey into the most authentic and vulnerable part of the human being. The heart is neither idealised nor portrayed as a perfectly harmonious place. Rather, it is the place where everything converges: what makes us strong and what makes us fragile, what we know about ourselves and what still remains to be understood.



**Technique and materials**

This work was created in 2012 using exclusively aniline dyes on polystyrene. The surface of the support is smooth and features no textural effects or relief. The technique enhances the fluidity of the colour, the transparency and the luminosity of the turquoise, whilst the golden lines define the visual interweaving of the composition.
