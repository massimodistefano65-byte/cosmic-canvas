
*"Sometimes all it takes is looking at the world from a different perspective to realise just how small our place in the universe is."*


**Description of the work**

*Another World* stems from a desire to evoke the perception of a distant, unknown place, observed from a perspective that seems to lie beyond the boundaries of everyday reality. The previous title, *Lunar Experience*, suggested a more specific interpretation; *Another World*, on the other hand, broadens the scope for interpretation, leaving open the possibility of another planet, another dimension, or simply a world viewed from a completely different distance.

The composition appears to depict a surface viewed from above. Smooth areas alternate with textured and spatula-finished sections, creating variations reminiscent of the geological formations, ridges and depressions of a celestial body. The material thus becomes an integral part of the representation: it does not merely describe a surface, but physically suggests its texture.

Black, anthracite grey and white dominate the image, almost completely eliminating any chromatic references to the terrestrial landscape. This choice accentuates the sense of distance, silence and suspension. We are not looking at a recognisable place, but at something that seems to belong to a remote space.

The bird’s-eye view introduces another dimension to the work: that of proportion. Observing a world from such a great distance inevitably means perceiving one’s own scale as infinitely small. It is a sensation that often arises when gazing at the night sky and the immensity of space, when one’s everyday perception is suddenly put into perspective.

In this sense, *Another World* represents not only a different place, but also a shift in perspective. Looking beyond the earthly realm means confronting something immensely greater and, at the same time, a form of silence that can inspire wonder, peace and contemplation.

The work also stems from the artist’s constant relationship with the sky and the observation of the universe – a daily presence that fuels curiosity, questions and the search for what we do not yet know. The sky thus becomes not merely a space to observe, but a threshold to a vaster reality.

The distinctive treatment of the surface, achieved in part through the use of polyurethane foam, helps to give tangible form to this vision. The various textural formations seem to belong to a remote realm, whilst the composition as a whole retains a deliberately undefined character.

*Another World* thus remains suspended between cosmic imagination, the observation of nature and a spiritual dimension: a fragment of an unknown world which, precisely through its immensity, invites the viewer to look beyond themselves.


**Technique and materials**

Work created in 2010 using mixed media on panel. The surface was treated with polyurethane foam, as well as earth pigments and acrylic paints, creating different textural forms and variations in relief that evoke the surface of a celestial body.
