*Even the soul, having borne the weight of existence, needs to rediscover playfulness, colour and lightness.*

**Description of the work**

*Funny Soul* represents a moment of respite within my artistic exploration. Whilst many of my works explore themes linked to memory, spirituality and introspection, this piece instead adopts a freer, more spontaneous and vibrant language.

The forms seem to chase one another without a rigid structure, allowing colour, gesture and energy to create the composition’s balance. The swathes of colour meet and clash, giving rise to a dynamic that conveys movement, enthusiasm and freedom of expression.

The title suggests a soul capable of smiling, of lightening its load and of rediscovering, even if only for a moment, the pleasure of existence. This is not a matter of superficiality, but of the realisation that light-heartedness possesses a profound value: it is a force that enables us to navigate the complexities of life without being completely overwhelmed by them.

This work is a luminous pause, an invitation to remember that play, spontaneity and joy are also part of the journey of inner growth.

**Techniques and materials**

The work is created on canvas using a mixed media technique combining acrylics and enamels. The layering of materials creates a surface rich in chromatic and gestural contrasts, where the paint retains its freshness, immediacy and freedom of expression.
