
An indissoluble interweaving of frequencies and connections, where the deep black encompasses a vibrant tapestry of pure energy.


### Meaning of the work

With *Connection for ever*, I sought to explore the idea of a total and enduring connection, an invisible yet tangible bond that unites every fragment of existence. The work is structured around a complex network of lines that overlap and intersect, evoking the structure of neural maps, cosmic circuits or organic tissues in constant communication.

The dense weave of white and dark marks interacts with a pulsating background of magenta and pink hues, a vibrant core of life that seems to be pushing to emerge. The deep darkness enveloping the edges of the work acts as a resonating chamber: it does not isolate, but rather encloses and intensifies the central energy, focusing the gaze on the infinite complexity of our inner and spiritual relationships with the universe.


### Technique and materials

On an 80x100 cm canvas, I worked by layering plaster and vinyl glues to create a textured, three-dimensional surface. I then combined the gestural quality of spray paint with the precision of acrylics, tracing the dense network of lines and defining the sharp chromatic contrast between the pulsating central area and the darkness of the edges.
