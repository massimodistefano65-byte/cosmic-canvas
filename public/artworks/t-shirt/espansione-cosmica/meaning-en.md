**"Cosmic Expansion" All-Over Print T-Shirt — Massimo Di Stefano Art**

Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist **Massimo Di Stefano** and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner impressions into works designed to be experienced through what we wear.

**The Work**

*Cosmic Expansion* is a visual outcry, an inner Big Bang. A glowing core of reds and magentas explodes outwards, shattering the cold, deep blues of the surrounding void through a dense network of radiant lines. 

For Di Stefano, this explosion represents the shattering of conventional structures and a rejection of silence: an alchemical fire that expands consciousness beyond all limits, defying the fear of insignificance.

**The primordial energy of the soul that shatters darkness to become light and space.**
