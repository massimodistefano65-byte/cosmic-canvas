
“Autumn Surprise” transforms an autumnal forest into an intense and luminous vision. Large golden leaves emerge amongst the tree trunks and dark foliage, whilst beams of light pierce through the woods, creating an atmosphere suspended between nature and imagination.

**The Work**

The yellow, ochre and orange leaves become fragments of light and symbols of change. At the centre, the luminosity seems to reveal a hidden energy, as if the forest were harbouring a surprise ready to unfold.

*Nature changes colour, and for a moment what was hidden becomes visible.*

**Press Release**

The design is reproduced in **Ultra HD All-Over Print**, designed to enhance the contrasts between the luminous leaves, the depth of the woodland and the beams of light across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
