
Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano[cite: 1] and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner feelings into works designed to be experienced through what we wear.  


### The Work

*The Song of the Sirens* is transformed into an extraordinarily dramatic and dreamlike garment. The translucent flows, hypnotic spheres and iridescent hues completely envelop the fabric, creating a fluid optical effect with a powerful aesthetic impact. A wearable work of art designed for those who love to stand out with an artistic, visionary and elegant style.

An explosion of geometric shapes and chromatic dynamism to be worn to express one’s identity.  


### Print Specifications

The design is reproduced in Ultra HD using All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation printing, enhancing depth, brightness and colour contrasts across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
