**‘When the Soul Is Joined to the Spirit’ All-Over Print T-shirt — Massimo Di Stefano Art**


This digital artwork transforms the union of soul and spirit into a luminous and dynamic design. White, light blue and golden strands radiate out from a central core, creating a web of energy against a dark background.

**The Work**

The circular centre represents a point of consciousness and contemplation. The lines expanding outwards evoke connections, movement and openness towards a broader dimension.

*When soul and spirit resonate, consciousness opens beyond its own boundaries.*

**Press note**

Design reproduced in **Ultra HD All-Over Print**, designed to enhance the contrast between the dark background, the central core and the luminous lines that criss-cross the surface of the T-shirt.
