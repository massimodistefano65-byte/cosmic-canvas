**"The Awakening Sound" All-Over Print T-Shirt — Massimo Di Stefano Art**

**Work by Massimo Di Stefano**



Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist **Massimo Di Stefano** and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner inspirations into works intended to be experienced through the clothes we wear.



**The Work**

*The Awakening Sound* translates the idea of a vibration capable of altering perception into an image. Circular shapes, arcs and geometric structures overlap around a central core, creating a composition suspended between the mental and cosmic realms.

The black and white accentuates the contrast between light, shadow and transparency, whilst the complexity of the forms produces an almost psychedelic effect.

**An invisible vibration that traverses the mind and opens up a different perception of reality.**



**Printing details**

The artwork is faithfully reproduced in **Ultra HD** using **All-Over Print (AOP)** sublimation printing technology, enhancing the contrasts, transparencies, details and complexity of the original composition across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
