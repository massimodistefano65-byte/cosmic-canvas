**“Cat Dream” All-Over Print T-Shirt — Massimo Di Stefano Art**

**Artwork by Massimo Di Stefano**



‘Cat Dream’ transforms the figure of a cat into a presence suspended between the city, nature and dreams. The interplay of settings and colours creates a mysterious, intense and richly detailed design.



**The Work**

The cat traverses a landscape composed of streets, trees, architecture and fragments of memory. Its presence represents an intuitive consciousness, capable of perceiving what normally remains hidden.

*In dreams, reality breaks down to reveal invisible connections.*



**Press release**

Graphic reproduced in **Ultra HD All-Over Print**, designed to enhance the transparencies, warm colours and the fusion between the animal figure, the urban landscape and nature.
