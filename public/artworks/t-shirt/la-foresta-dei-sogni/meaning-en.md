**Every design originates from an original artwork by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano and from his ongoing research in digital art. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner suggestions into artworks designed to be experienced through clothing.**



*The Forest of Dreams* invites the viewer into a suspended space between reality and imagination, where nature takes on a symbolic language and dreams become an inner journey. The chromatic contrasts and mysterious atmosphere evoke a landscape capable of inspiring deep emotions and ever-changing interpretations.

Rather than depicting a real forest, this design suggests a place of consciousness where intuition, memory and transformation coexist. Wearing it means carrying with you the fascination of mystery and the call toward what is still waiting to be discovered.



**Print note**

The artwork is reproduced in **Ultra HD** using **All-Over Print (AOP)** sublimation technology, enhancing every chromatic detail of the original artwork across the entire surface of the garment.
