Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano and his ongoing exploration of digital art. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner feelings into works designed to be experienced through the clothes we wear.



“Blind in the Storm” is a digital artwork by Massimo Di Stefano transformed into a wearable design. The image brings the same visual energy of the original artwork to the fabric: a vortex of red, white, black and grey, composed of lines, fragments and tensions in motion.



\*\*The Work\*\*



This composition represents an inner storm: a moment in which thoughts, emotions and impulses overlap to the point where all sense of direction is lost.



The red appears as a vital and urgent force, whilst the diagonal and radial lines evoke wind, bursts of energy and fragments of perception. The gaze is drawn into a vortex where chaos and transformation coexist.



\*When everything seems chaotic, even the loss of direction can become the beginning of change.\*
