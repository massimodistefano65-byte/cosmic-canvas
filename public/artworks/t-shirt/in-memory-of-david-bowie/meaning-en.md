
Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano[cite: 1] and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner feelings into works designed to be experienced through what we wear.  


### The Work

*In memory of David Bowie 10 - 01 - 2016* transforms the garment into a piece of clothing with a charm that is both rock-inspired and cosmic. The abstract, three-dimensional pattern of the original artwork covers the entire surface of the T-shirt, highlighting the energy of the geometric grids, the orange-cyan contrasts and the deep shadows. A T-shirt designed for those who wish to wear art as a form of tribute and free expression of identity.

An explosion of geometric shapes and vibrant colour to wear as a way of expressing your identity.  


### Press release

The artwork is reproduced in Ultra HD using All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation printing, enhancing depth, brightness and colour contrasts across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
