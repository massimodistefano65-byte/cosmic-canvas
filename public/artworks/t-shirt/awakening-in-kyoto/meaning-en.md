**Artwork by Massimo Di Stefano**

Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist **Massimo Di Stefano**, transforming images, visions and perceptions of nature into works of art that can also be experienced through what we wear.


**The Artwork**

*Awakening in Kyoto* captures a woodland shrouded in shadow, suddenly bathed in golden light. Ochre and orange leaves emerge from the deep green and black, whilst rays of light radiate out from the centre like a revelation.

The image transforms a natural landscape into a perceptual experience: a moment in which what was already there suddenly seems to become visible.

**An awakening of the gaze, in the heart of nature.**


**Print details**
