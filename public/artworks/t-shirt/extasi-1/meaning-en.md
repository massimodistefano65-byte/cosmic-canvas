T-Shirt All-Over Print "Extasi I" — Massimo Di Stefano Art

*Artwork by Massimo Di Stefano*

Every design originates from an original artwork by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano and from his ongoing research in digital art. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner experiences into artworks designed to be experienced through clothing.

*The Artwork*

Extasi I captures the moment when consciousness seems to move beyond its ordinary boundaries, opening to a state of profound inner expansion. Shapes and vibrant colors evoke a continuous flow of energy, inviting the viewer to follow intuition rather than logic.

*Print Note*

The artwork is faithfully reproduced in Ultra HD using All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation technology, enhancing every chromatic detail while preserving the expressive intensity of the original composition across the entire garment.
