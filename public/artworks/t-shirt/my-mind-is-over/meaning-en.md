**“My Mind Is Over” All-Over Print T-shirt — Massimo Di Stefano Art**



Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist **Massimo Di Stefano** and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner impressions into works designed to be experienced even through what we wear.



“My Mind Is Over” transforms a nocturnal urban setting into a dynamic and surreal vision. Lights, signs, architecture and coloured lines seem to move towards the observer, representing the intensity of a mind overwhelmed by too many stimuli.



**The Work**

The city becomes a reflection of inner overload. Distortions and radial movement evoke thoughts, signals and impulses that multiply, whilst cool colours are enlivened by flashes of pink, red and purple.

*When the mind goes beyond its limits, even chaos can become a new form of vision.*



**Printing details**

The artwork is faithfully reproduced in **Ultra HD** using **All-Over Print (AOP)** sublimation printing technology, enhancing the contrasts, transparencies, details and complexity of the original composition across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
