Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner impressions into works designed to be experienced through the clothes we wear.



### The Work

*Forgotten hopes* transforms the garment into a bold and enigmatic graphic composition with a powerful contemporary impact. The complex digital pattern of the original artwork unfolds across the entire fabric, highlighting the intense vibrancy of the magenta-red tones, the blue reflections and the dense black vector grid. Wearing this T-shirt means showcasing a piece with a strong, refined character, rich in conceptual tension.

An explosion of geometry and chromatic dynamism to wear as an expression of one’s identity.



### Printing details

The artwork is reproduced in Ultra HD using All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation printing, enhancing depth, brightness and colour contrasts across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
