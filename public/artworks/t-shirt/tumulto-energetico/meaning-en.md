
Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist Massimo Di Stefano and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner feelings into works intended to be experienced through what we wear.  


### The Work

*Tumulto energetico* transforms the garment into a true statement of strength and expressive power. The rich, textured quality of the original artwork is rendered across the entire fabric, highlighting the visual contrast between the fiery reds, the teal veins and the latticework of luminous lines. Wearing this T-shirt means carrying with you the overwhelming energy of a dynamic, profound and uncompromising work of art.

An explosion of colour and texture to wear as an expression of one’s inner strength.  


### Print Specifications

The artwork is reproduced in Ultra HD using All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation printing, enhancing the depth, brightness and colour contrasts across the entire surface of the T-shirt.
