**"Close to the soul" All-Over Print T-Shirt — Massimo Di Stefano Art**

**Work by Massimo Di Stefano**

Each design is inspired by an original work by the Italian visual artist **Massimo Di Stefano** and his ongoing artistic exploration. His creations transform visions, emotions and inner impressions into works designed to be experienced through what we wear.

**The Work**

*Close to the soul* is much more than an image: it is a gateway to the artist’s inner abyss. A magnetic vortex that, from shades of teal, plunges into a glowing red core – a symbol of a primordial energy that refuses to be silenced. 

Di Stefano transforms the surface into a map of the soul, an alchemical impulse that defies the fear of the void. The work is an invitation to look beyond the obvious, to find that invisible and authentic vibration that makes us profoundly human.

**A vortex of energy and truth that connects the innermost depths of the soul with the vastness of the universe.**
