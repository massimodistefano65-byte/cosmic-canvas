A dreamlike architecture in which visual memory resurfaces, revealing the enigmatic tension between the tangible outline of reality and the vibrations of the unconscious.


### Meaning of the work

*The Recurring Dream* explores the psychological dimension of the visual fragment that returns insistently to the mind, defying time and the logic of wakefulness. The work is structured around a stark formal contrast: in the foreground, the dense and enigmatic silhouette of the dark branches acts as a natural barrier, a filter through which the viewer looks into the depths of their own psyche.

Behind this organic grid, a magmatic universe of vivid, contrasting colours unfolds: incandescent bursts of yellow and red intertwine with sombre tones of purple, blue and spectral glimmers. This fluid and mysterious backdrop embodies the living substance of dreams that continue to resurface, an inner landscape suspended between unease and fascination. The work does not seek to interpret the dream, but captures its persistence, transforming an intimate experience into a powerful symbolic image.


### Technique and materials

The work arises from an elaborate, layered process that blends natural photography with complex digital manipulation and painting techniques. The visual effect is enhanced by an Ultra HD Fine Art print on premium card, capable of rendering maximum sharpness both in the detailed silhouettes of the branches and in the colour saturation of the luminous flows in the background.
