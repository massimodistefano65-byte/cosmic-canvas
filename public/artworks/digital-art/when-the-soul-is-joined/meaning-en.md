
*When soul and spirit meet, consciousness becomes a bridge between matter and that which transcends it.*

**Description of the work**

“When the Soul Is Joined to the Spirit” presents a composition dominated by luminous lines, filaments and trajectories that converge towards a central core. The image appears as an expanding space, traversed by subtle forces that intertwine, entwine and seem to draw every element back towards a point of origin.

At the centre lies a circular form, resembling an eye, an iris or an energy diagram. Its concentric structure creates a focal point within the image’s complexity. Around this core, the white, light blue and golden lines unfold like fibres, veins, waves or connections traversing the space.

The relationship between the centre and the surrounding lines is fundamental. The central core suggests concentration and presence, whilst the filaments seem to represent the outward expansion of energy. The work thus brings together two opposing yet complementary movements: inner contemplation and openness towards a broader dimension.

The dark background, criss-crossed by shades of red and purple, creates an almost cosmic depth. This is not an empty space, but a dense and vibrant dimension in which light gradually manages to manifest itself. The contrast between the darkness and the luminous filaments lends the image an intense atmosphere, suspended between mystery and revelation.

The title refers to the union between soul and spirit. In the work, this union does not appear as a defined figure or a narrative scene, but as a process of connection. The lines seem to form an invisible network linking the centre to the periphery, the individual being to a vaster dimension.

The soul can be recognised in the most intimate and secluded part of the image, whilst the spirit seems to manifest itself in the movement of the lines that extend beyond the boundaries of the core. Their encounter produces a form of dynamic equilibrium: not an obliteration of matter, but its permeation by an immaterial force.

“When the Soul Is Joined to the Spirit” therefore represents a moment of integration. It is the instant in which that which is internal and that which belongs to a broader dimension resonate with one another. Consciousness is not depicted as something isolated, but as a living centre embedded within a network of connections.

**Technique and materials**

A work created using **Digital Art**, through the digital construction of luminous lines, concentric shapes, filaments, textures and layers of colour.

The composition utilises the contrast between the dark background and the luminous trajectories to create an abstract and dynamic vision. The central core organises the movement of the image and transforms the ensemble of signs into a symbolic representation of the connection between soul, spirit and energy.
