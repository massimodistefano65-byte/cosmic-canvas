*In the dream, the cat does not merely observe the world: it traverses its hidden layers and listens to what reality cannot express.*

**Description of the work**

“Cat Dream” arises from the superimposition of different places, images and levels of perception. The figure of the cat occupies the centre of the composition as a silent, suspended presence, almost a guardian straddling the boundary between the real world and the world of dreams.

Urban settings, architecture, trees, people and fragments of landscape intertwine around the animal. The images are not arranged according to a single perspective: they overlap and interpenetrate, like memories resurfacing during sleep or scenes from different places brought together by perception.

The cat appears sharper than the other elements and becomes the focal point of the work. However, even its form is permeated by transparencies and interferences. It is not completely separated from its surroundings: it seems to merge with them, as if it belonged simultaneously to the street, to nature and to a mental dimension.

The animal’s presence introduces a sense of mystery and alertness. The cat is a creature capable of moving in silence and perceiving what escapes the ordinary gaze. In this work, it can be interpreted as a symbol of an intuitive consciousness, vigilant even within a dream, capable of traversing different realms of reality without needing to explain them rationally.

The composition is dominated by warm, brown, green, golden and purplish tones. This choice of colours lends the image a nocturnal, suspended atmosphere, though not one of complete darkness. Light filters through the various layers of the image, allowing fragments of everyday life, nature and memory to emerge.

‘Cat Dream’ does not simply depict a cat dreaming. Rather, it suggests that the dream is a place where images of reality can shift position, merge and reveal connections that are normally invisible. The city becomes a mental landscape, nature becomes memory and the figure of the animal becomes a presence capable of guiding the viewer’s gaze through this complexity.

The work explores the relationship between consciousness and perception, between what we see and what we sense. In the dream, reality does not disappear: it transforms, layers itself and reveals its hidden side.

**Technique and materials**

The work is created using **Digital Art**, through the digital superimposition and blending of photographic images, urban and natural environments, textures and colour treatments.

The composition uses transparency and layering to create a scene in which multiple visual realities coexist within the same space. The final image thus takes the form of a dreamlike landscape, suspended between photography, memory and imagination.
