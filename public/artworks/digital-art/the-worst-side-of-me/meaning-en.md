**The meaning of the artwork**
Unfiltered immersion into the shadow zones of consciousness. An exploration of the fragmented parts of the psyche: complex thoughts, chaotic emotions, and those "broken" aspects that usually remain submerged. It is the courage to look at what hurts, recognizing identity precisely within the disorder.

**Technique and material**
Sharp planes and lashing overlaps give body to inner conflict. Digital matter layers into contrasting textures that do not seek harmony, but the truth of friction. A restless beauty born from the acceptance of one's most difficult side, transforming the wound into a visible structure.

- **Theme:** Archetypes of the shadow and fragmented psyche.
- **Language:** Complex digital stratifications, unpacked planes.
- **Concept:** The dignity of being "broken".

*There is a deep strength in inhabiting one's own ruins.*
