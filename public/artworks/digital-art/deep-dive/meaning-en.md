Deep Dive

2019

*"Every true dive does not lead to the bottom of the sea, but to the deepest part of oneself."*

**Description of the work**

*Deep Dive* stems from a desire to depict that moment when consciousness gradually leaves the surface of thought to venture into a quieter, deeper dimension. It is not a literal dive into the sea, but an inner journey in which the clamour of the world fades away, allowing insights, memories and perceptions that normally remain hidden to emerge.

The composition suggests a fluid, suspended space, where matter, light and energy seem to merge into a single breath. The intense shades of turquoise and blue evoke the tranquillity of the ocean depths, whilst the coppery and golden hues emerge like fragments of memory or traces of an invisible presence that permeates the entire scene.

The curved lines and textures running through the work recall natural movements, currents and vibrations that belong not only to water, but also to thought. Each element seems to emerge and vanish in the same instant, suggesting that the deepest reality cannot be observed with a superficial glance, but only experienced through lived experience.

This work speaks to the value of immersion as an act of understanding. The further one moves away from the clamour of the surface, the more possible it becomes to encounter an authentic part of oneself. It is an invitation to slow down, to listen to the silence and to discover that it is precisely in one’s inner depths that the clearest insights can arise.

**Technique and materials**

The work was created using digital art and printed in high-quality Fine Art format, preserving the rich colours, the depth of the textures and the delicate interplay of light, matter and visual effects found in the original composition.
