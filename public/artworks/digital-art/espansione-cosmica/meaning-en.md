*An explosion of light and matter in which consciousness shatters its own boundaries to become the universe.*

**Description of the work**

Visually, the work appears as a dynamic spherical core, torn apart and criss-crossed by an infinite network of fibrous, radiating lines. A stark contrast between the electric blues and abyssal blacks of the periphery frames an erupting centre of incandescent reds, oranges and magentas. But for the artist, this is not merely an astrophysical representation. 

*Cosmic Expansion* is the exact visual translation of that inner tension between what one is and what one could be, a desperate call to break free from conventional structures. The central sphere acts as a psychological boundary, whilst the beams of light represent the ‘fire capable of transforming inner matter’, an alchemical energy that refuses to remain walled up alive. In this explosion, Di Stefano probes the void and fills it with the voice of his own Shadow: a primordial, ravenous and pulsating presence that demands to be seen in its absolute, unrepeatable truth.

**Technique and materials**

Through digital manipulation, Di Stefano creates a visual paradox of immense impact: the cold pixel is warped to simulate an organic tension, almost muscular and nervous. The lines that cleave through space act as gateways, transforming the digital medium into a threshold where invisible light bursts forth forcefully from the dark matter of the background.
