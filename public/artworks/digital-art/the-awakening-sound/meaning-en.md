*"Sometimes awakening does not come through a word, but through a vibration capable of altering the way we perceive our surroundings."*



**Description of the work**

*The Awakening Sound* is a digital work constructed by superimposing circular shapes, geometric structures and curvilinear forms that appear to radiate out from a central core. The image is dominated by white, black and a wide range of greys, creating an atmosphere suspended between a cosmic dimension, an organic structure and mental perception.

At the centre of the composition lies a complex interweaving of forms that is simultaneously reminiscent of a cellular structure, a transforming organism and a crystalline configuration. Around this core, large arcs and transparent surfaces unfold, like waves expanding into space and traversing different levels of the composition.

The choice of a monochrome palette is particularly significant. By almost completely eliminating colour, the work focuses on the relationship between light, shadow, transparency and superimposition. The forms thus seem to belong to a dimension that is not strictly physical, but rather mental and perceptual.

The title *The Awakening Sound* introduces the idea of a vibration capable of bringing about an inner change. The sound is not represented literally: it is evoked through the propagation of the forms, the concentric circles and the trajectories that seem to traverse the image. It is as though an invisible frequency had brought about a transformation of space and, at the same time, of perception.

The composition also maintains a strong ambivalence between order and complexity. The geometric shapes appear to follow a precise structure, but the numerous overlaps produce an almost psychedelic sensation, in which the eye can continually discern new connections and new paths.

In this sense, the work ties in with the artist’s exploration of consciousness and the perception of reality. ‘The Awakening’ can be understood as the transition from ordinary perception to a state in which that which normally remains invisible, or simply goes unnoticed, begins to take on a presence.

*The Awakening Sound* does not, therefore, represent a specific sound, but seeks to transform into an image the very idea of a vibration capable of opening up a new perception. The result is an abstract and immersive composition, suspended between matter and energy, geometric order and cosmic suggestion.



**Technique and materials**

Work created in 2016 using **digital processing techniques**. The composition arises from the superimposition and manipulation of geometric shapes, circular structures, transparent elements and digitally processed textural surfaces.

The use of black and white, along with numerous intermediate shades, allows for the creation of depth, luminosity and transparency without resorting to colour. The superimposition of different layers creates a complex visual structure, in which the forms appear to expand and propagate through space.

The digital technique thus becomes an integral part of the expressive exploration, enabling the creation of a visual reality that cannot be reduced to a naturalistic representation and is particularly suited to evoking vibration, energy and an alteration of perception.
