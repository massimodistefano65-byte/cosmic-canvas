An intense architecture of the unconscious, where fragments of forgotten expectations and desires remain entangled in a dense web of chromatic and structural tensions.


### Meaning of the artwork

*Forgotten hopes* takes the form of a profound visual meditation on the fragility of desires and the human soul’s tendency to let its hopes settle or be set aside over time. The work rejects any purely decorative intent, transforming the digital space into a force field dense with psychological meanings. The composition is permeated by a tangle of spherical forms and facets that evoke bubbles of memory or fragments of suspended aspirations: nuclei of light and colour attempting to emerge from a vibrant background of magenta-red tones and cyan flashes.

This vital energy is held in check by a complex and enigmatic network of dark, layered lines, which acts as a formal and emotional cage. This dialectic between the pulsating energy of the luminous nuclei and the suffocating rigour of the grid embodies the conflict between the impulse to realise one’s dreams and the external or internal resistances that keep them dormant. The loss of hope is not depicted as an absence, but as a living, painful and imprisoned presence waiting to be evoked by the observer’s gaze.


### Technique and materials

The work stems from extensive research in the field of digital art, characterised by a complex, layered superimposition of geometric shapes, light refraction effects and high-definition vector structures. The graphic result is enhanced by an Ultra HD Fine Art print on high-quality card, capable of rendering with extreme clarity both the deep saturation of the red and blue tones and the meticulous detail of the interlaced lines.
