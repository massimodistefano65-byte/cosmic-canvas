Extasi I
2014

*Ecstasy is not an escape from reality, but the moment when consciousness expands beyond its ordinary boundaries and begins to perceive the invisible.*

*Artwork Description*

*Extasi I* was created to represent a state of consciousness in which the separation between the inner and outer worlds seems to dissolve. Rather than depicting a mystical experience in a religious sense, it expresses a profound moment of perceptual openness where energy, emotion, and intuition merge into a single vision.

Its flowing forms and vibrant colors appear to be crossed by a living force in constant transformation. Light emerges from within the composition as an energy that shapes space and suggests a dimension beyond what can be seen. Nothing remains static; every element participates in a continuous movement that reflects the transformative processes of consciousness.

The artwork encourages the viewer to move beyond a purely rational perspective and embrace intuition as another way of knowing. In this artistic research, ecstasy is not an escape from life but a deeper state of presence, capable of connecting human beings with their innermost self and with the mystery of the universe.

Like much of my work, *Extasi I* explores the ongoing dialogue between matter and spirit, between what can be observed and what can only be perceived. It is an invitation to recognize that the most meaningful transformations often arise from experiences that cannot be explained, only lived.

*Technique and Materials*

Created as a digital artwork and printed as a Fine Art edition, preserving the original composition's chromatic depth, luminosity, and richness of detail.
