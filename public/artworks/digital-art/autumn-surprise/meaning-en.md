
*When autumn sweeps through the forest, nature’s memory is awakened and what seemed hidden suddenly appears.*

**Description of the work**

“Autumn Surprise” arises from the transformation of a natural landscape into a vision suspended between reality, memory and imagination. The forest occupies the entire composition with its dark trunks, intertwined branches and dense foliage, yet is traversed by large golden leaves that seem to appear in the foreground as unexpected presences.

The autumn leaves are not merely decorative elements. They seem to emerge from the forest like fragments of light, memories or images preserved within the natural world. Their warm colour – a blend of yellow, ochre and orange – contrasts with the dark greens and the shaded areas of the woodland, creating a strong tension between darkness and revelation.

At the centre of the work, a luminous area opens up from which beams of light seem to radiate. This point introduces an outward movement and suggests that the surprise hinted at by the title is not merely visual. It is as though the forest harbours a hidden energy, ready to manifest itself suddenly through colour and light.

The composition brings together two different depths: the physical depth of the woodland, formed by trunks, leaves and branches, and the inner depth, generated by the overlapping layers and the central luminosity. The space thus appears transformed into a place of apparition, where the landscape is not merely observed but seems to reveal an invisible presence.

‘Autumn Surprise’ speaks of seasonal change, but also of nature’s capacity to surprise the consciousness. Autumn is not presented merely as a loss or the end of a cycle. The leaves changing colour become a sign of transformation: that which breaks away and changes can, for an instant, glow with a new intensity.

The work invites the viewer to look more slowly. Within a seemingly familiar scene, something manifests itself without warning: a light, a memory, a sense of connection with nature. The surprise thus becomes a moment of perceptual awakening, in which the visible world seems to open up to a deeper dimension.

**Technique and materials**

This work is created using **Digital Art**, through the superimposition and digital transformation of natural images, photographic elements, colour textures and light effects.

The composition blends the depth of the forest with enlarged autumn leaves and beams of light, creating an image that lies somewhere between an enhanced photograph, a digital collage and an imaginative vision. The digital language alters our perception of the landscape and transforms nature into a symbolic and contemplative space.
