
*A beam of the purest light between the weight of matter and the infinity of the void.*

**Description of the work**

The work is based on a minimalist and extreme visual composition, split by a sharp and dynamic diagonal line. The upper right-hand section is dominated by absolute black, a deep and unfathomable abyss that absorbs the gaze. As a counterbalance, in the lower left-hand corner, a dense, cold and textured surface extends: the grainy textures and anthracite tones evoke the solidity of bare stone or the roughness of a mineral landscape.

Separating these two dimensions is a white line, bright and sharp. More than a mere geometric boundary, it appears as a raised flap, a tear in the fabric of reality. It is the perfect visual embodiment of the title: that fragile, luminous ‘thin layer’ which divides the material chaos of human existence from the unfathomable immensity of the cosmos or the deep unconscious. A work that evokes a sense of vertigo, suspension and anticipation.

**Technique and materials**

Through digital art, the artist creates a striking contrast in texture. The masterful use of textures simulates the tangible porosity of organic matter, contrasting it with the impenetrable flatness of black. The extremely high definition of the white cut creates an almost three-dimensional illusion of spatial laceration.
