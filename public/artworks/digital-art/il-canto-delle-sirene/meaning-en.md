### Meaning of the work

*The Song of the Sirens* explores the subtle threshold where the perceptible world dissolves into the allure of dreams. The work draws inspiration from the Homeric myth of the irresistible call, transforming it into a purely sensory and psychedelic experience. The composition is dominated by fluid, translucent layers, iridescent beams and cosmic spheres that act as veritable visual catalysts: hypnotic eyes and centres of gravity that draw the viewer into the image.

The interplay of light and transparency reproduces the vibration of an invisible sound frequency, an echo that bewitches and blurs the boundaries of reality. The sirens’ call is not intended here as a threat, but rather as an invitation to abandon the certainty of the tangible and plunge into the depths of the unconscious, allowing oneself to be carried away by a harmonious flow of organic forms and luminous flashes.


### Technique and materials

The work is characterised by a complex interplay of fractal geometries and nuances of optical refraction. The quality of the Ultra HD Fine Art print on high-grade card guarantees an exceptional rendering of the overlapping transparencies, enhancing the colour range that spans from warm amber tones to sidereal blues and emerald greens.

*Notes on presentation:* The artwork is available both as a single Fine Art print on card (29 x 39 cm) and complete with a mat, frame and protective glass (total dimensions 42 x 52 cm), ready for display.
