A cosmic explosion of memory and visual matter, conceived in the moment of parting as a tribute to the immortal legacy of an iconic figure.


### Meaning of the artwork

*In memory of David Bowie 10 - 01 - 2016* was born out of the emotional impact of a symbolic date, transforming the grief at the artist’s passing into a powerful visual elegy. The work is structured as a veritable celestial clash: a galaxy of geometric shapes, typographic fragments, dotted grids and spectral flashes that evoke the White Duke’s eclectic and chameleon-like cosmic imagery.

The composition is permeated by a profound dynamic tension, in which warm, orange-hued tones blend with cosmic darkness and cyan flashes. The digital imagery seems to disintegrate and reassemble, evoking interstellar travel and the persistence of art beyond physical life. This is not merely a commemoration, but a celebration of the indelible mark left by an icon who has shaped contemporary culture and the artist’s personal imagination.


### Technique and materials

The work is developed through a complex digital layering process, combining geometric vector structures, image distortion effects and high-resolution textures. The colour rendering is enhanced by an Ultra HD Fine Art print on archival-quality card, capable of reproducing the utmost clarity in the microscopic details of the grid and the vibrancy of the dark and light tones.

*Notes on presentation:* The work is available either as a single Fine Art print on card (30 x 40 cm), or complete with a mat, frame and protective glass (total dimensions 42 x 52 cm), ready for display.
