**The Series: Dream**
This work is part of a series entitled ‘Dream’. It is designed to be hypnotic, acting as a vortex or portal that draws the viewer into the subconscious. Dreams are non-linear journeys in which we lose our usual bearings; the spiral represents this transition from the physical world to the depths of our inner vision. It is a movement of the soul towards the centre of the self.

**Dream 6**
An absolute contrast between light and shadow. Geometric forms accelerating towards a magnetic centre, nullifying the perception of ordinary space. A tunnel of frequencies where the mind surrenders to the rhythm of the depths. Here, the dream becomes architecture of pure energy: an invitation to plunge into the origin of one’s own thoughts.

*The point where silence becomes vision.*
